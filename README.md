# SQL Projects Portfolio

A collection of SQL projects demonstrating my skills in data exploration, relational queries, and business analysis. Topics include customer conversion, marketing attribution, subscription churn, revenue, and user engagement.

## Projects

### [SQL Analysis Portfolio](./SQL_Analysis_Portfolio)

Business analyses covering three areas:

- **Funnel analysis:** Track conversion from quiz participation to home try-on and purchase.
- **Marketing attribution:** Explore first-touch, last-touch, and purchase attribution by source and campaign.
- **Churn analysis:** Calculate monthly subscription churn overall and by customer segment.

Includes SQL queries and a churn analysis presentation.

### [SQL Business Metrics](./SQL_Business_Metrics)

Queries for measuring sales performance and player activity.

- Daily orders and revenue.
- Product revenue shares and reorder rates.
- Daily active users and average revenue per user.
- Next-day player retention.

### [SQL Multiple Tables Project](./SQL_Multiple_Tables_Project)

Four exercises focused on combining related tables:

- **Lyft:** Trips, riders, and autonomous vehicles.
- **Reddit:** Users, posts, and subreddit activity.
- **VR Startup:** Employee assignments and staffing needs.
- **Welp:** Business ratings and customer reviews.

### [Codecademy Learners Data](./Codeacademy_learners_Data_Project)

Explore learner profiles and course progress.

- Learner counts by email domain and location.
- Mobile app usage and hourly sign-up activity.
- Course progress across learner groups.

### [Twitch Data Analysis](./Twitch_portfolio_porject)

Analyze viewing behavior and chat participation.

- Popular games and geographic viewing patterns.
- Video player usage and game genres.
- Hourly views in Brazil.
- Chat participation rates by game.

## SQL Skills

- **Data exploration:** `SELECT`, `DISTINCT`, filtering, and sorting.
- **Aggregations:** `COUNT`, `SUM`, `AVG`, `MIN`, and `MAX`.
- **Multiple tables:** Inner, left, and cross joins, plus `UNION`.
- **Query structure:** Subqueries and common table expressions (CTEs).
- **Conditional logic:** `CASE` statements and null handling.
- **Business metrics:** Conversion, retention, churn, and revenue calculations.

## Getting Started

1. Choose a project folder and read its overview.
2. Review the SQL queries and any included datasets.
3. Load the required data into a compatible SQL environment.
4. Run individual queries to explore the analysis.

Dataset availability varies by project. Several queries use SQLite syntax and may require adjustments for other database systems.

## Purpose

Build practical SQL skills by turning business questions into queries and exploring patterns in customer, product, and activity data.
